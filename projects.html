Sticker Artist & Content Creator Website — Client Project

I built a website for a client who creates custom stickers and sells them on Etsy, while also producing video content for YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. The site works as a clean, modern blog-style portfolio where visitors can explore their artwork, learn about the creator, and access direct links to all their social media platforms.

My goal was to give the artist a centralized hub that showcases their work, highlights their personality, and makes it easy for fans and customers to find their content across different platforms.
